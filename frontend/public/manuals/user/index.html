1. Signing in

Most workers are auto-provisioned by their site admin — you'll receive an SMS or email with a sign-in link. Open the link on your phone.

  1. Open the Paneltec Civil app.
  2. Tap Sign in.
  3. Enter the email address your admin registered for you.
  4. Enter the temporary password from your onboarding message and tap Continue.
  5. Set your own password when prompted (min 8 characters, mix letters and numbers).
ℹ
If you don't have credentials yet, ask your site supervisor — you'll appear in the Simpro-synced worker list within a few minutes of being added.

2. Home tab

The Home tab shows four action tiles above your workday tasks:

Scan QR

Opens the camera to scan asset tags, site gate QRs, or worker cards. Fastest way to log meter readings, start a pre-start, or record who's on site.

New Pre-Start

Starts a pre-operational safety check on any plant or vehicle assigned to you. Photos, signatures, and defect notes are captured inline.

Incident Report

Report an injury, near-miss, or hazard. Photos are attached automatically to the form.

Sign On

Sign on to the current site for the day. Records your arrival time + GPS on the site diary.

3. Forms tab

The Forms tab lists every form your admin has assigned to your role. Categories run down the left; scroll to find the one you need.

  1. Tap a form card to open the form runner.
  2. Fill each field. Required questions are marked with a red asterisk.
  3. For checklists (like Service Check Sheets), tap the trinary buttons: ✓ Pass, ✗ Fail, or NA Not applicable. Add notes on any failed item.
  4. Sections group related questions. Tap a section header to collapse or expand it. Sections with unanswered required questions show an ATTENTION badge; completed sections show COMPLETE.
  5. Sign at the bottom and tap Submit.
ℹ
Drafts auto-save every 30 seconds. If you lose signal mid-form, keep filling it in — everything queues locally and syncs the moment you reconnect.

4. SWMS viewer & acknowledgement

Every SWMS your role covers is listed on the SWMS tab. Tap any card to read the document; scroll to the bottom to acknowledge.

  1. Open a SWMS. The full document renders inline — no separate PDF download needed.
  2. Read all sections. Each section is chunked so you can page through on a phone.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, tap I acknowledge, then sign.
  4. Your acknowledgement is timestamped and available on the admin dashboard immediately.

5. Docs tab (SDS & reference library)

The Docs tab now surfaces every shared reference folder your admin has flagged as worker-visible. This includes:

  • SDS (Safety Data Sheets) — chemical safety data for anything you'll handle on site.
  • Chemical Storage & Handling
  • Australian Standards
  • Toolbox Talks
  • Company Policies, Manuals & Procedures
  • WHS / Environment / Quality / Management procedure sets

Tap any folder to open it, then tap a file to view or download.

6. Site QR & visitor sign-in

Every site has a QR sticker at the gate. Anyone (worker or visitor) can scan it to sign in:

  1. Point your phone camera at the site QR.
  2. Tap the notification. If you're already signed in, you'll go straight to the site kiosk. If you're a visitor with no app account, you'll see the visitor sign-in form.
  3. Fill in name, company, host, purpose, vehicle rego (optional). Tick the safety induction acknowledgement.
  4. Tap Sign in. You'll see a welcome screen with site-specific safety info.
ℹ
If a scanner shows a login prompt instead of the visitor form, the phone may still have an old cached login. Close the app, clear browser cookies for the site (see Common issues), then re-scan.

7. Ask AI

Tap the sparkle icon in the top bar of any tab to open Ask AI. Use it to:

  • Look up compliance details for a task ("What does AS 2601 say about excavation shoring?").
  • Draft a SWMS entry, incident narrative, or toolbox talk from a couple of sentences.
  • Interpret an SDS ("What's the recommended PPE for this chemical?").

Close the panel with the ✕ button in the top-right or by tapping the backdrop.

8. App updates & offline drafts

Updates

When a new APK is available you'll see an amber Update available banner at the top of the app. Tap Update now to download and install; the app will reopen on the new version.

Offline drafts

Every form auto-saves as you type. If you lose reception mid-form, keep working — drafts are stored on the phone. The moment reception returns, submissions sync automatically. You can see queued drafts in the Forms tab under Pending.

9. Signing out

  1. Open the Profile tab (bottom-right icon).
  2. Tap Sign out at the bottom of the profile screen.
  3. Confirm. All local drafts sync before sign-out completes.

10. Common issues

Stale login / stuck on sign-in page

iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data. Reopen the app.

Android Chrome: Chrome → Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → "All time" → tick Cookies + Cached images → Clear data.

SDS folder empty

Fixed in v.132km. If you still see an empty SDS folder, force-refresh the app (pull down on the Docs tab), then contact your admin.

"Access Restricted" screen on a form

Certification gates are DISABLED by default in the current build. If you see this screen, an admin has explicitly re-enabled the gate for that form and the global feature flag is on. Ask your supervisor.

"Previous crash detected" toast

The app recovered gracefully — tap Dismiss. Your work was preserved. If it repeats within a session, sign out and back in.

Preview mode caveats

If you're viewing the app inside the admin preview panel as a "Generic preview user", per-user overrides (site pinning, personal certs) don't apply. Real workers see the correct scoped data on their own phone.
