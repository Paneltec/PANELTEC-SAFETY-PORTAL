1. What personal information we collect

  • Worker identity: name, email, phone, employment role, employer / company link.
  • Licences & certifications: type, number, expiry, issuing authority, uploaded scan or photo.
  • Compliance activity: signed-in pre-starts, sign-ons, sign-offs, toolbox meetings, SWMS reviews, site inductions, hazard and incident reports, risk assessments and site diary entries. This includes field responses, notes, photos, signatures and GPS location where you permit it.
  • Photos and signatures captured on-device via camera and signature pad.
  • Device identifiers for provisioning: device ID, model, OS version, app version.
  • Session and authentication: hashed passwords, magic-link tokens, session cookies.
  • Fleet and vehicle usage (via the Navixy integration): asset assignment, service history, tag associations.
  • Backup portal metadata: agent heartbeat, delivery timestamps, NAS destination.

2. Why we collect it (lawful bases)

  • Legal obligation. The Work Health & Safety Act 2011 (Cth), the Work Health and Safety Act 2012 (Tas) and equivalent state legislation require accurate records of workplace safety activity, incidents, worker inductions and competencies. Records are typically retained for 5–7 years depending on record type.
  • Contract. To provide the WHS compliance service to Paneltec workers and subcontractors as part of their employment or engagement.
  • Legitimate interests. Safeguarding workers, defending against WHS claims and maintaining operational continuity.

3. Who we share it with

  • Emergent (emergent.host) — platform infrastructure host. Provides managed database, backups and hosting. Production data resides on Emergent-managed infrastructure with backups and point-in-time recovery.
  • Navixy (navixy.com) — GPS fleet tracking integration. Only vehicle metadata and tag data flows in and out.
  • Simpro (simpro.com) — worker records integration. Only worker demographic data flows in and out.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic — used for AI-generated safety briefings and PDF extraction. Prompts are anonymised; no personal identifiers are persisted by the third party.
  • Regulators, insurers and courts — only where legally required (for example, WorkSafe Tasmania incident notification).

We do not share personal information with advertising partners, analytics resellers or data brokers.

4. Where data is stored

  • Emergent-managed infrastructure — production database and application servers.
  • Local encrypted NAS in Paneltec's office (14.41 TB UGREEN tower) — used as a backup destination.
  • Some of our infrastructure providers operate outside Australia. Where data is transferred offshore for hosting or AI processing, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles personal information consistently with the APPs.

5. How long we retain data

  • Active worker records: retained for the duration of engagement plus 7 years.
  • Compliance forms (pre-starts, SWMS, incidents, inspections, hazard reports, site diaries): 7 years from submission.
  • Backup snapshots: retained for 7 days rolling on the primary platform and indefinitely on the local encrypted NAS.
  • Data is deleted at the end of the retention period or on validated user request, subject to legal-hold exceptions.

6. Your rights under the APPs

  • Access. You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction. You may ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out-of-date or incomplete.
  • Complaint. You may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
  • To exercise these rights, contact accounts@paneltec.com.au.

7. App permissions used and why

  • Camera — capturing photos for pre-start evidence, incident documentation and licence / certification uploads.
  • Storage / Files — saving and loading form drafts, viewing document library files and cached photos.
  • Location — attaching GPS coordinates to incident reports and site sign-ons only while the app is open and you are capturing a record. Not used in the background.
  • Internet — syncing with the WHS platform backend.
  • Notifications — reminders for overdue compliance items. Optional; you can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Install unknown apps — used for the Android sideload distribution path during closed testing. Will be removed once Google Play distribution is live.

8. Security measures

  • Data in transit: TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Data at rest: encrypted at the storage layer via Emergent-managed infrastructure.
  • Access control: role-based (Admin, Worker, Viatec, External Contractor).
  • Audit logging on all administrative actions.
  • Session tokens are signed, short-lived and refresh on activity.
  • Per-device provisioning (QR-pair). Sessions cannot be copied across devices.

9. Cookies and trackers

  • The mobile app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • The web app uses only functional cookies (authentication session). No marketing cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL. Material changes will trigger an in-app notice on the next launch. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
