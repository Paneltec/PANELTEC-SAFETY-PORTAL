# Feature Graphic Brief — 1024×500 px

## Specs
- Dimensions: exactly **1024 × 500 px**
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- Google bans: no screenshots inside, no "NEW" badges, no sale prices

## Design Direction

**Background**: Solid Paneltec navy `#1B3D66` — clean, professional, matches the app's screen background

**Left side** (40%): Paneltec Civil wordmark in white — the "A" chevron icon in blue `#2C6BFF` — same as the app's login screen branding

**Right side** (60%): Tagline in white, large weight — one of:

### Tagline Options (pick one)
1. `Field & Safety, sorted.` — casual Australian tone, implies the app handles both
2. `Your crew's WHS command centre.` — positions it as the hub, not just another app
3. `Safety compliance from the field.` — straightforward, keyword-rich for search
4. `Jobs. Forms. Safety. One app.` — punchy list, scannable at small size
5. `Built for crews, not cubicles.` — differentiator, personality

**Accent**: Thin orange `#F97316` stripe or dot cluster at bottom-right corner — subtle brand touch, breaks the navy monotone

**Typography**: Space Grotesk Bold for tagline (matches app's display font), white `#FFFFFF`

## What NOT to do
- No app screenshots (Google policy)
- No star ratings or "Download now" CTAs
- No busy backgrounds or gradients
- No text smaller than ~24pt equivalent at 1024px width
