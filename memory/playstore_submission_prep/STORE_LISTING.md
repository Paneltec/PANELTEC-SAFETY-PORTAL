## Full Description

Paneltec WHS is the field companion for civil contracting teams who take Work Health & Safety seriously. It replaces paper forms, WhatsApp job messages, and scattered spreadsheets with a single app built for workers wearing gloves, standing in the sun, and moving between sites every day.

**How it works**
Your office dispatches today's job via SMS. Open the app and your assignment is waiting — address, scope, SWMS, and site-specific forms pre-loaded. Sign on to site with GPS verification, complete your pre-start and daily forms, snap hazard photos that get auto-classified by AI, and request leave without chasing payroll.

**Key features**
• SMS job dispatch — receive today's assignment automatically, no checking emails
• Digital forms library — pre-starts, permits, inspections, toolbox talks with colour-coded categories
• Hazard & incident reporting — photograph a hazard; AI suggests severity, controls, and description
• Leave requests — request annual, sick, or long-service leave from the field; track approval status
• GPS site sign-on — prove you're on-site with location-verified check-in
• Asset management — view fleet vehicles and equipment assigned to your crew
• WHS document library — SWMS, SOPs, and safety policies accessible offline
• AI-structured site diary — type rough notes, let AI format them into a professional diary entry

**Who is it for?**
Paneltec WHS is a private business app for teams managed through the Paneltec Civil platform. You need a Paneltec account and PIN code provided by your employer to log in. It's used by civil contractors, road crews, rail maintenance teams, utility workers, and their supervisors across Australia.

**Built for the field**
Large tap targets for gloved hands. Works on low-bandwidth connections. Designed for phones carried in high-vis pockets — no tiny buttons, no multi-step menus.
